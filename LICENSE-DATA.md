# Data licence

Everything under `data/`, `raw/` and `seeds/` is licensed CC BY-SA 4.0.

The Aramaic text is *Sefer ha-Zohar* from Hebrew Wikisource
(https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/ספר_הזהר), Mantua pagination,
licensed CC BY-SA 4.0. Revision ids and fetch dates are in `data/SOURCES.md`.

Seed lists cite Moshe Cordovero, *Pardes Rimonim* (Gate 23) and Joseph
Gikatilla, *Sha'arei Orah*, both public domain.

The translations in `data/translations.v*.jsonl` and the interpretations in
`data/interpretations.v*.jsonl` were written by the model
Claude Fable 5.1 (Anthropic) from that text and are shared under the same
licence, CC BY-SA 4.0. They are model output, not reviewed by
specialists.

# Changelog

## release 0.1.0 — 2026-10-11 (zohar.digital)
- First public release: the site at https://zohar.digital, bilingual EN/ES, on tables 0.1.0 (below).
- Symbols, sefirot, the Tree (Kircher's three-pillar layout, double-line paths), Read (folio by folio, navigation above and below), Scholars (method, strata, strongest pairs with Aramaic labels, exports, citation).
- Translations: 678 surfaced passages in English and Spanish (1,356 texts) by Claude Fable 5.1, Batch API, prompt t3; $73.30 in all including the pilot. Each row carries model, prompt version, date and a source hash; stale rows are dropped at build; hand corrections in `data/translation_overrides.tsv`; a "report a problem" link per translation.
- Keys panel on every passage page: each image in reading order, its usual sefirah and the source of that assignment.
- Home opens with Zohar III 152a (the garment of the Torah); Radiance logo.
- Deferred work: docs/BACKLOG.md (interpretations pilot done, full run parked).

## tables 0.1.0 — 2026-10-11
- First full build of the Zohar main body (3 volumes, Mantua pagination) from Hebrew Wikisource: 29 parasha pages pinned in `seeds/wikisource_revisions.json`, 1,630 folio-sides, 15,909 passages, 832,463 tokens. The separate Wikisource page `זוהר השמטות` (the appendix printed at the end of the Vilna/Izmir editions) is not fetched; appendix text the Wikisource editors copied into the parasha pages is kept as stream `addendum` (220 passages on 12 folio-sides, III 92b–287b; the one at III 92b is Sefer ha-Bahir text by the editors' own note).
- Text streams: Wikisource labels the parallel streams of each folio (`רע"מ דף …`, `מדרש הנעלם דף …`, `סתרי תורה דף …`, …). Every label is classified (main / stream / overlay) or the build fails; a plain `דף` label takes its stream from the level-2 heading that governs it (Bo and Mishpatim Ra'aya Meheimna, 19 sections). Passages per stream: main 13,505; Ra'aya Meheimna 1,667; addendum 220; Midrash ha-Ne'lam 214; Sitrei Torah 171; Tosefta 64; Raza de-Razin 48; other (the `ד"א` baraita in Sifra di-Tseniuta) 11; Matnitin 9. A stream's paragraphs continue the folio-side's numbering after the main stream (`3.088b.002` may be Ra'aya Meheimna); ids have a three-digit paragraph field.
- Strata: a non-main stream is its own stratum; main-stream passages take the ranges in `seeds/strata.tsv` (Tishby): Idra Rabba, Idra Zuta, and the unlabelled Shemot Midrash ha-Ne'lam (II 4a–5b, 14a–21b; Vaera main begins on 22a). Main 12,545; Ra'aya Meheimna 1,667; Idra Rabba 655; Midrash ha-Ne'lam 378; addendum 220; Sitrei Torah 171; Idra Zuta 141; Tosefta 64; Raza de-Razin 48; other 11; Matnitin 9 passages.
- Symbol seed: 139 symbols, 361 surface aliases (Cordovero gate 23, Gikatilla, Zohar usage); every seeded root occurs in the corpus (checked at build). 138 have at least one mention; `mouth_of_river` (פומא דנהרא) has none.
- Mentions: 54,003; 5,825 co-occurrence pairs (PMI over all passages; two symbols tagging the same tokens are rivals, not partners); 257 state rows over 6 declared states (table `state_decl`).
- Surfaced passages: score (2·own + 1·partner mentions) / (tokens + 20), passages of at least 12 tokens first.
- Tokens: 70.5% with root; 21.6% known non-lexical (empty-root override: function words, divine names, abbreviations); 7.9% unknown (no root). Confidence ≥ 0.8: 53.8%.
- Text fixes: templates that carry Zohar text (gematria, abbreviation expansions, underlined or enlarged words) are kept instead of dropped; heading lines, horizontal rules, an editors' gematria table, image links, editor's placeholder notes ("this folio has no Ra'aya Meheimna", 379 sections) and paragraphs without a Hebrew letter are dropped; 20 one-off Wikisource typos (templates and section tags) are patched in `pipeline/segment.py` with a comment each.
- Known gaps: an earlier note listed 31 folio-sides as absent; most were folio-sides that carry only a stream section (e.g. III 275b–282b, Ra'aya Meheimna) and are now present. Within each volume's first-to-last folio-side (I 1a–251a, II 2a–269a, III 2a–299b) only III 116a–116b have no passage in any stream: the Wikisource pages never mark folio 116. Ohr HaChamah, Mikdash Melekh and Yahel Or (Sefaria) have no comment on III 116 either, 115b is of ordinary length and Bamidbar opens at 117a, so this is most likely a skip in the printed numbering, not lost text (not checked against a scan). II 1a–1b and III 1a–1b, also listed before, lie outside those ranges and have no passage; whether the printed edition carries Zohar text there was not checked. The fetch report lists the folio-sides above 3× the median token count: III 151a, 193b, 199a, 280b, each carrying addendum text.
- Seed decisions: earth: Hebrew ארץ excluded as alias (bare Hebrew ארץ is overwhelmingly verse quotation; the dictionary measures Zohar usage), so earth counts Aramaic ארעא only: 1,145 mentions in 765 passages (was 2,019).
- Seed decisions: teshuvah: labelled Teshuvah / Teshuvá (was Return / Retorno). The Zohar reads it as tashuv-he, the return of the he; a plain gloss loses that. Label-only change, counts unchanged.
- Not yet: Zohar Chadash, Tikkunei Zohar, glosses.

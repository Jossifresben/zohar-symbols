# Changelog

## tables 0.1.0 — 2026-10-11
- First full build of the Zohar main body (3 volumes, Mantua pagination) from Hebrew Wikisource: 29 parasha pages pinned in `seeds/wikisource_revisions.json`, 1,630 folio-sides, 15,912 passages, 832,567 tokens. `זוהר השמטות` excluded.
- Text streams: Wikisource labels the parallel streams of each folio (`רע"מ דף …`, `מדרש הנעלם דף …`, `סתרי תורה דף …`, …). Every label is classified (main / stream / overlay) or the build fails. Passages per stream: main 13,865; Ra'aya Meheimna 1,530; Midrash ha-Ne'lam 214; Sitrei Torah 171; Tosefta 64; Raza de-Razin 48; Matnitin 9; other (the `ד"א` baraita in Sifra di-Tseniuta) 11. A stream's paragraphs continue the folio-side's numbering after the main stream (`3.088b.002` may be Ra'aya Meheimna); ids have a three-digit paragraph field.
- Strata: a labelled stream is its own stratum; main-stream passages take the Idra ranges from `seeds/strata.tsv` (Tishby). Main 13,060; Ra'aya Meheimna 1,530; Idra Rabba 655; Midrash ha-Ne'lam 214; Sitrei Torah 171; Idra Zuta 150; Tosefta 64; Raza de-Razin 48; other 11; Matnitin 9 passages.
- Symbol seed: 139 symbols, 362 surface aliases (Cordovero gate 23, Gikatilla, Zohar usage); every seeded root occurs in the corpus (checked at build). 138 have at least one mention; `mouth_of_river` (פומא דנהרא) has none.
- Mentions: 54,877; 5,834 co-occurrence pairs (PMI over all passages; two symbols tagging the same tokens are rivals, not partners); 257 state rows over 6 declared states (table `state_decl`).
- Surfaced passages: score (2·own + 1·partner mentions) / (tokens + 20), passages of at least 12 tokens first.
- Tokens: 70.5% with root; 21.6% known non-lexical (empty-root override: function words, divine names, abbreviations); 7.9% unknown (no root). Confidence ≥ 0.8: 53.8%.
- Text fixes: templates that carry Zohar text (gematria, abbreviation expansions, underlined or enlarged words) are kept instead of dropped; heading lines, image links, editor's placeholder notes ("this folio has no Ra'aya Meheimna", 379 sections) and paragraphs without a Hebrew letter are dropped; 20 one-off Wikisource typos (templates and section tags) are patched in `pipeline/segment.py` with a comment each.
- Known gaps: an earlier note listed 31 folio-sides as absent; most were folio-sides that carry only a stream section (e.g. III 275b–282b, Ra'aya Meheimna) and are now present. Within each volume's first-to-last folio-side (I 1a–251a, II 2a–269a, III 2a–299b) only III 116a–116b have no passage in any stream: the Wikisource pages never mark folio 116. II 1a–1b and III 1a–1b, also listed before, lie outside those ranges and have no passage; whether the printed edition carries Zohar text there was not checked. The fetch report lists 27 folio-sides above 3× the median paragraph or token count; four are far above the median token count (III 151a, 193b, 199a with 3,784 tokens, 280b) and probably hold a missed folio boundary.
- Not yet: Zohar Chadash, Tikkunei Zohar, translations, glosses.
